Experimental 5X/6X capability extension
Based on the DLSSG-X6-experimental attachment by pipotoufikxyz-lgtm:
https://github.com/sdli1995/dlssg_for_sm86/discussions/95
Original repository: https://github.com/pipotoufikxyz-lgtm/dlssg_for_sm86
This distribution combines its CPU frame-count extension with our Native024 Fix1 texture-view correction. The original model and GPU kernels are unchanged. Original authors and third-party rights remain unchanged. Experimental support is an upper limit requested by the game, not a new DLSS model or a guarantee of game compatibility.


Native 0.2.6 is a local compatibility-scheme version, based on Native 0.2.4,
not an upstream model update. Stable and experimental proxies include local
Fix1 texture handling, Vulkan resource interoperability/synchronization,
shared-transfer and reuse optimizations, lazy D3D12 loading and process-lifetime
retention. The embedded 310.1 model and inference kernels are unchanged.
The initial R2/SM86 payloads are retained unchanged.
